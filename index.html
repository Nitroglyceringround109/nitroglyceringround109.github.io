🎨 Customizing jeff with Adapters

One of jeff's coolest features is its ability to use LoRA adapters. These are small files that fine-tune jeff's knowledge for a specific domain. Here's how they work:

  • Base model: jeff comes with general decision-making abilities.
  • Adapters: You can download specialized adapters (e.g., "marketing", "finance", "health", "travel") from the jeff community or create your own.
  • Swapping: To switch adapters, open jeff's settings menu and select the adapter you want. It takes just a second — no restart needed.

Think of it like changing the glasses on your face — same brain, but different lenses for different situations. This keeps the model small and fast while still being versatile.
